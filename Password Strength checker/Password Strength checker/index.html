Cybersecurity Best Practices

Length Over Complexity:

Passphrases with 4+ random words (e.g. correct-horse-battery-staple) are harder to crack and easier to remember.

Never Reuse Passwords:

A leak in one service compromises all accounts sharing identical credentials.

Use a Password Manager:

Store cryptographically unique passwords securely without memorizing hundreds of codes.

Enable Multi-Factor (MFA / 2FA):

Adds a critical second line of defense even if your password becomes compromised.
